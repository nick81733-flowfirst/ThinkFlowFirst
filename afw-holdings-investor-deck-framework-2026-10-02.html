Slide 18 · Capital Efficiency

Own the Platform — Not Every Chair

A conventional clinic rollout requires the parent company to fund leases, fit-outs, staff, local management, working capital and customer acquisition. Our international model is different.

Local partners fund much of the downstream physical expansion. AFW concentrates capital on demand generation, supply, brand, training, partner systems, standards and commercial support.

Scale the system, not the real estate.

Slide 22 · Why USD 6 Million?

A Balanced Entry Valuation

Asset-backed foundationAFW is not a concept-stage startup. The group already has strategic land, facilities, accommodation and wellness infrastructure.
Operating businessThe Sanctuary already exists and has a credible pathway toward more than USD 1 million of annual mature revenue before broader commercialization.
Embedded growth optionsCambodia Commercial, International FlowFirst, the demand engine, brand, commercial systems and future property development.

Why not USD 3M?

At USD 3 million, the valuation would largely reflect today's physical assets and operating business while assigning little value to the commercial platform already being developed.

Why not USD 10M?

A higher valuation may become supportable once Cambodia economics, international replication and the broader platform are more fully proven.

USD 6 million seeks to recognize what has already been built while preserving meaningful upside for investors who are taking the next-stage execution risk.
