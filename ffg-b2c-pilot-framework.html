Think Flow First · FFG · Team working framework

14-Day Direct Enquiry
Acquisition Pilot

Facebook & Instagram → WhatsApp → personal conversation. Three markets, one number, manual handling first.

Prepared 19 October 2026 · Version 1.0 · For digital marketing and market leads · Working plan, subject to local advertising and clinical review

Decision summary

What we are doing—and not doing

Primary objective: generate relevant, voluntary enquiries quickly from people interested in stroke recovery information, caregiver support or evidence-based risk reduction, and help them reach an appropriate person.

Meta advertisement → “Send WhatsApp Message” → central WhatsApp Business number → manual triage → regional representative → one-to-one information / suitable clinician consultation

Agreed: launch a 14-day test concurrently in Singapore, Kuala Lumpur and Phnom Penh; begin with Facebook and Instagram; test three themes; handle enquiries manually and review results.

Not in phase 1: ManyChat subscription, new B2C CRM build, webinars as the primary acquisition engine, TikTok rollout, or automated medical advice. Retain ManyChat and CRM integration as later options.

2 · Creative strategy

Test three themes. Produce one static image and one 15–30-second captioned video per theme (six core creatives), then localize copy, language, phone CTA and context for the three markets. Use warm, realistic visuals and Asian faces; avoid distressing patient imagery, implied diagnoses or dramatic before/after results.

CTA: Send WhatsApp Message
B · Family & caregiver“Supporting someone after stroke? Explore practical questions families can discuss with their rehabilitation team.”
CTA: Send WhatsApp Message
C · Risk awareness“Stroke prevention starts with understanding risk factors. Learn the evidence-based steps that matter.”
CTA: Send WhatsApp Message

Creative rule: ad copy must not imply the viewer has had a stroke or knows someone with one. “Comment FLOW” can remain an optional organic engagement tactic, but the paid pilot's primary CTA is direct messaging.

3 · Platform and targeting

Channel: Meta Ads Manager, testing Facebook and Instagram placements. No TikTok in the first test. Use separate location-based campaigns or ad sets for Singapore, Greater Kuala Lumpur and Phnom Penh, with locally appropriate languages and permitted broad adult age ranges.

Narrow by message and geography, not assumed illness. Avoid targeting based on sensitive health characteristics, uploaded patient lists, inferred stroke status or retargeting people by medical condition. Review current Meta policies before launching any health-related advertising.

Illustrative budget, pending approval: S$50 per market per day × 3 markets × 14 days = S$2,100 total. Split the initial spend broadly across the three themes, then adjust only after enough actual conversations have been reviewed. This is a test budget, not an approved commitment or a forecast.

4 · Manual WhatsApp enquiry handling

Use a dedicated official business number if practical; ensure the person managing it can respond promptly during advertised hours. Configure a basic greeting, away message and saved quick replies. Test that each ad opens WhatsApp with the correct number and that a person can successfully send the first message.

Suggested first response

Thank you for contacting Think Flow First. We share educational information about stroke recovery and risk reduction. Would you like information on (1) recovery and rehabilitation, (2) supporting a family member, or (3) reducing stroke risk? Which country are you contacting us from?

Handover

Coordinator tags market and interest, confirms permission to connect the person to a local team member, records an enquiry ID and follow-up owner, and sends a brief acknowledgement. Aim to respond within one hour during published service hours; define an escalation backup for unattended enquiries. One-to-one conversations should be informative, not high-pressure sales.

Medical boundary: no diagnosis or personalized treatment advice from marketers. Sudden stroke signs require immediate emergency services, not a WhatsApp consultation. Emerging Advanced Flow/IGDS information may be discussed only with transparent disclosure of evidence gaps, risks and legal status; never describe it as proven to treat or prevent stroke, heart disease or cancer.

5 · Tracking without a new CRM

Begin with a restricted-access tracking sheet; do not place diagnoses, medical histories, screenshots of private chats or sensitive patient information in general marketing sheets.

7 · Scorecard and decisions

Core metrics: spend; WhatsApp conversations started; valid enquiries; response within one hour; consultations requested; consultations scheduled; consultations completed; qualified customer outcomes where lawful; cost per valid enquiry and per consultation. Break all figures down by market, creative and placement.

Do not mistake engagement for demand. Clicks, comments, views and messages are secondary. Track drop-off between opening WhatsApp and actually sending a message.

Automation trigger: reconsider ManyChat only when manual handling creates repeated delays or sustained meaningful volume (illustratively 10–20 genuine enquiries per day across the three markets). This is a review threshold, not a commitment.

8 · Decisions the team must finalize before launch

  1. Who owns the central number and daytime response rota, including backups?
  2. What is the approved test budget per market, and which currency/account pays?
  3. Which languages, landing context and local contacts are appropriate for each city?
  4. Who signs off health claims, clinical handover, privacy and jurisdiction-specific advertising compliance?
  5. Where will the restricted-access enquiry sheet be maintained?

Operating principle: fast, respectful access to useful information—not promises of recovery or disease prevention. Test demand first. Automate later.
