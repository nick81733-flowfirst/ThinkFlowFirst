01 / Introducing Advanced Flow

A new frontier in IV therapy

Advanced Flow is an intravenous infusion approach using hydrogen- and oxygen-based Integrated Gas Delivery System (IGDS) technology.

An emerging field of investigation

The technology is being explored in relation to gas delivery and physiological processes. Scientific plausibility does not establish clinical benefit.

Professional exploration

Review the scientific information, evidence limitations and proposed clinical framework.

Responsible implementation

Clinical use must follow applicable local authorisations, independent medical judgement and appropriate safeguards.

02 / Our philosophy

06 / Clinic requirements

Built around professional medical care.

Open to licensed general practitioners, specialists, IV therapy clinics and healthcare establishments.

Appropriate authorisations

Valid professional and facility licences; product and clinical use clearance.

Qualified clinical staff

Medical oversight and trained infusion personnel.

Safe clinical environment

Suitable facilities, monitoring and emergency preparedness.

Clinical governance

Assessment, informed consent, records and adverse-event reporting.

Professional onboarding

Review of evidence, local requirements, training and readiness.

Clinical independence

The treating doctor retains responsibility for patient suitability, clinical decisions and care.
