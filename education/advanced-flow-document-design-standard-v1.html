DejaVu Sans regular / bold. A5: 12 mm margins, 25–31 pt titles, 10–11 pt body, 7–9 pt labels. A4: 16 mm margins, 28–34 pt titles, 11–12 pt body. Use one column on A5; two columns only for short parallel content.

04 / Asset discipline

Use locked source artwork

Advanced Flow: LOGO-AF-002_Transparent.png for covers; LOGO-AF-004_Horizontal.png for running headers. Think Flow First: LOGO-TFF-002_Transparent.png for relevant sections.

Preserve original artwork

Maintain aspect ratio, colours and tagline; use transparent variants on light backgrounds. Minimum clear space: 10% of visible logo height. Never redraw, stretch, recolour or add new text inside a logo.

Use authentic photography selectively

Choose approved photographs relevant to the message. Do not present illustrative or AI-generated images as actual facilities, clinicians or patients. Obtain permission for identifiable people.

Keep content modular

One main message per page; use repeated headings, short body blocks, evidence notes and consistent footer codes. Separate international core from country-specific annexes.

Record and review

List source filenames and versions, check legibility and page boundaries, confirm clinical and regulatory wording, and obtain approval before publishing.
