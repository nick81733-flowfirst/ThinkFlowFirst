ECMP & Clinical Partner Framework

Standalone working paper for discussion with Dr. Mah and prospective stakeholders. Examines wholesale economics, clinic incentives, network development, patient-source accounting, and unresolved implementation matters.

Exploratory only — not an approved agreement, approved rate card, medical claim, or authorization to market a treatment.

1. Executive proposition

FFG proposes a layered distribution model: FFG → Exclusive Country Marketing Partner (ECMP) → Area Distributor → Partner Clinic. Existing clinics would use available clinical capacity, earning clinical fees for network patients and potentially retaining genuine retail margin on their own business. The ECMP may develop a national network of existing doctors rather than construct new centres.

Dr. Mah is an ECMP contender; this document is intended to identify assumptions, negotiate commercial terms, and record open questions, not to pre-commit either party.

Who retains the RM240?

Own-customer clinic sale: the clinic buys the product at RM400, invoices its own patient and, subject to legally permitted resale, keeps the unused acquisition allowance. At RM800, its RM400 (US$100.00) retail spread includes clinical service value; after RM14 (US$3.50) estimated supplies, potential cash contribution is RM386 (US$96.50) if existing staff have spare capacity.

Network-directed sale: the authorised commercial network may use up to RM240 (US$60.00) for customer acquisition. The administering clinic receives RM160 (US$40.00) clinical service revenue, or potentially RM146 (US$36.50) cash contribution after RM14 supplies with existing staff capacity.

Illustrative network split: 5% area/network developer (RM40 / US$10.00) + 25% direct commercial channel (RM200 / US$50.00) = 30% of retail. Such commissions must not operate as inducements for doctors to refer or prescribe.

6. Territory development and settlement

The ECMP develops participating clinics and Area Distributors. Business development, professional education and onboarding should be paid for genuine services. A proposed commercial 5%/25% split is an economic model, not an approved doctor referral arrangement.

Inventory: two settlement paths

Clinic-originated customer: clinic may buy stock at RM400 (US$100.00) per infusion, hold inventory and collect its own retail price, if permitted by product and facility licensing.

Network-directed customer: consider supplier-owned or consigned stock with a separate RM160 (US$40.00) clinical-service payment, to avoid requiring clinics to finance inventory for outside patients. Physical custody, invoicing, stock traceability, dispensing and cash reconciliation need an agreed documented procedure.

Potential ECMP boundaries

Discuss Malaysia territory scope, clinic onboarding responsibilities, existing FFG contacts, FFG-owned clinics, earned exclusivity tied to measurable milestones, product supply and minimum commitments. None of these terms is concluded in this document.

7. Decisions required before adoption

  1. Confirm RM400 (US$100.00) supply price and legal buyer of record at each distribution tier.
  2. Decide who bills patients and finances stock for each customer channel.
  3. Agree permitted commercial functions and compensation; exclude physician referral kickbacks and related-party circumvention.
  4. Validate product regulatory pathway, clinic authority, advertising permissions, sterile preparation, product handling and pharmacovigilance.
  5. Test RM800 (US$200.00) market acceptance, retail variation and taxes.
  6. Determine ECMP territorial obligations, minimum sales commitments and FFG outlet carve-outs.
  7. Confirm payment security, returns, losses, quality procedures and dispute handling.
Compliance boundary: A wholesale margin, territory override or marketing fee does not by its label make a transaction lawful. No patient-linked compensation to a medical practitioner or related party should be launched without independent Malaysian legal and medical-ethics review. This framework does not substantiate clinical efficacy or product authorization.
