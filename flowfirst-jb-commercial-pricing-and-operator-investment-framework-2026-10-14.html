Current agreed working terms for the Singapore-to-Johor Bahru customer platform. This document supersedes the earlier JB pricing, separate nurse-fee and 25%-marketing financial cases. Figures are planning illustrations, not audited earnings or guaranteed returns.

Locked working structure: All-inclusive S$1,550 / S$2,900 / S$5,400 packages. S$30 per infusion represents nursing administration and standard supplies and is excluded from every sales commission and marketing-budget calculation. Allocate 15% first line, 5% second line, 5% combined Patricia Sim and Rykel override, and 5% general marketing, all calculated on the therapy-only package component.

3. Two distinct businesses and investable boundaries

Upstream: FFG Technology, Product Supply & Malaysia Distribution

FFG product transfer to the Malaysia exclusive country master partner (ECMP): US$50 per infusion; assumed FFG production cost US$20. Malaysia ECMP transfers to the JB operator at US$75 per infusion. Thus the combined FFG + ECMP gross supply spread is US$55 per infusion before any separately agreed upstream costs. This entire upstream pool is retained outside the JB investor equity.

Downstream: FlowFirst JB — Singapore Market & Outlet Operations

Singapore-facing customer acquisition, all-inclusive package sales, booking, customer support, participating clinic contracts and marketing operations. Initially FFG-controlled, potentially open to minority investors. Supplies are purchased at US$75 per infusion; contracted independent JB clinics deliver the clinical services. Territorial rights should cover the JB Singapore-customer model only and be documented.

4. Corrected 20-infusion operator contribution

Confirmed commercial term, to be documented in clinic contract: the clinic receives a total of RM200 per infusion, all funded from the all-inclusive customer package, rather than collecting another RM100 directly from the patient. 7. Execution controls before scale or fundraising

  1. Determine the lawful Malaysian product and clinic delivery route before accepting treatment bookings.
  2. Define FFG upstream vs FlowFirst JB downstream commercial rights, term, territorial scope, supply transfer prices, governance and termination safeguards in contracts.
  3. Implement the agreed RM200 all-in clinic payment in a signed agreement specifying clinical supervision, nurse administration, standard supplies, monitoring and facility costs; document any additional separately billable medical services.
  4. Publish all-inclusive pricing consistently across Singapore and JB sales materials; do not advertise S$30 as a second fee.
  5. Record commissions against the therapy-only component and separate expense-based marketing budgets from commissions actually payable; independently review clinical referral restrictions.
  6. Refresh the 60-month fundraising cash-flow and investor IRR model using the current all-inclusive package prices, 30% therapy-only marketing expense, tax, volume ramp, and capital requirements.
