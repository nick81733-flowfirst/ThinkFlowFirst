Malaysia Demand Creation · 4 October 2026

Malaysia NOVO Demand Creation
Digital Marketing Team Operating Brief

Day-to-day operating brief for testing whether Think Flow First can reliably create enough qualified demand to support one repeatable 2-chair Advanced Flow / NOVO partner unit in Malaysia.

Companion document to the Malaysia NOVO Market Entry, Demand Creation & Home-IV Playbook

1. The Mission

Can Think Flow First reliably generate about 10 new 20-session customers per month for one 2-chair partner location?

The marketing team's job is not to maximise followers or cheap leads. It is to discover a repeatable acquisition system that produces qualified customers at workable acquisition cost.

2. Commercial Target

2 chairsRepeatable pilot unit
10 customers/monthNew programme customers
20 sessions/customerCurrent commercial model
200 sessions/monthDemand produced by the unit

Base planning uses a 75-minute session slot. At the working retail assumption of US$150/session, one 20-session programme represents US$3,000, and 10 new customers represent approximately US$30,000 monthly programme value.

3. Category We Are Building

We are not positioning NOVO as another generic IV drip.

THINK FLOW FIRST
Trillions of cells. One circulation.

People already think about vitamins, NAD, stem cells and longevity interventions. Our marketing introduces another question: what about circulation and the cellular environment supporting everything else?

5. Condition-Based Campaigns Come Later

Potential future doors include stroke, cardiovascular concerns, cognitive decline, metabolic health, Parkinson's and recovery. These require tighter medical-claims and regulatory controls. Do not begin disease-specific NOVO advertising without formal approval.

6. Public Campaign Goal: Sell Curiosity First

Existing belief → Curiosity → Flow First education → Advanced Flow interest → Human conversation.

The first ad does not need to explain NOVO. It should create the thought: “I have never considered health this way before.”

7. Advertisement Structure

1. HookInterrupt an existing belief.
2. New questionIntroduce flow or delivery.
3. FrameworkThink Flow First.
4. CTAInvite education or self-assessment.
Do not lead with “Buy NOVO now.” The product comes after education and qualification.

8. Initial Digital Funnel

Advertisement / Content → Think Flow First Landing Page → Self-Identification → Flow First Education → Advanced Flow Introduction → Qualification → WhatsApp / Phone Conversation → Appropriate Access Path

Self-identification should ask what brought the prospect: NAD/longevity, vitamin IV, regenerative medicine, healthy ageing, circulation concern, stroke concern, caring for someone, or other.

11. High-Risk Claims — Do Not Publish Independently

  • “NOVO treats stroke.”
  • “NOVO cures cancer.”
  • “NOVO reverses Parkinson's.”
  • “NOVO reverses Alzheimer's.”
  • “NOVO prevents heart attacks.”
  • “NOVO regenerates organs.”
  • “NOVO makes you younger.”
  • “NOVO is approved in Malaysia.”

Do not imply Malaysian approval, guaranteed clinical results, universal special access, or regulatory clearance because NAD/vitamin drips are already sold in the market.

12. Home-IV Rule

The team is researching Malaysian home-IV sellers to understand consumer behaviour, pricing, demand and last-mile delivery—not to imitate questionable practices.

Preferred future model: Licensed provider → Patient assessment → Controlled product → Trained nurse → Patient home → Central records & adverse-event process.

16. What Counts as a Qualified Lead?

  • Can access the intended service geography.
  • Understands this is private-pay.
  • Has genuine interest in advanced health interventions.
  • Is willing to speak with a medical/Flow First representative.
  • Is comfortable with IV administration or willing to understand it.
  • Could potentially complete a programme.
  • Has reasonable financial capacity.
  • Does not expect guaranteed disease treatment.
CPQL matters more than CPL.

19. Creative Testing Method

Start with 4 acquisition doors × 3 concepts = 12 controlled tests. Record spend, impressions, clicks, CPC, leads, qualified leads, CPQL, consultations, customers and CAC.

Kill weak messages. Improve promising messages. Scale winners.

21. Retargeting

People who watch substantial Flow First video content, visit multiple education pages, complete self-assessment, open Advanced Flow content, join webinars or start WhatsApp contact should enter a progressive educational retargeting sequence. Do not simply repeat the same sales ad.

22. Home-IV Intelligence

Map approximately 20 Malaysian home-IV operators. Capture account, city, following, clinic affiliation, operator type, products, NAD availability, prices, home fees, frequency, claims, contact method, product photos, comments, engagement and offer structure.

This is competitive intelligence, not permission to copy medical claims.

23. Weekly Marketing Meeting

  1. Which acquisition door produced the most qualified leads?
  2. Which hook generated the lowest CPQL?
  3. Which audience produced the highest consultation attendance?
  4. Which campaign produced actual customers?
  5. What did we learn that changes next week's spending?
The ultimate score is new programme customers.

25. Final Principle

We are not trying to convince Malaysia that it needs another drip.

We are trying to create a new health question:

What about flow?

The sequence is:

Curiosity → Education → Trust → Qualification → Access

And the brand that should own that question is:

THINK FLOW FIRST.
