Development History

This page preserves major working versions of Think Flow First so stakeholders can review how the product evolved and use the sequence as a practical record of web-app development.

V2 · 6 Oct 2026 · Content & trust upgrade

Make the idea understandable and credible

V2 kept the working responder journey from V1 but strengthened the educational and institutional layer so the site could begin to feel like a serious trust-building destination rather than only a functional prototype.

  • Deeper explanation of what Flow First means and why sequence matters
  • Expanded “Why Flow Matters” education
  • Substantially deeper RAHO story and evidence framing
  • New About / Trust page
  • New Contact page
  • Stronger visual hierarchy and presentation
  • Final intent step changed from single-choice to multi-select
  • Development-history link added to the main site

What this version is for: review whether the content now explains the idea clearly enough, whether RAHO gives sufficient depth and credibility, and whether the overall site feels trustworthy enough before moving to multilingual and voice.

V1 · 6 Oct 2026 · Functional prototype

Prove the responder journey

The first working version focused on function rather than depth or visual polish. It demonstrated the end-to-end responder flow from an entry door into education, the seven-question intake, contact capture, intent selection, sharing, and campaign-tag preservation.

  • Entry doors for Stroke, Heart Disease, Parkinson's, Prevention, Stem Cells, Longevity/Biohacking, Overseas Treatment and Recovery
  • Basic Flow First explanation and early RAHO credibility layer
  • Seven-question guided intake
  • Contact capture
  • Three follow-up intentions, initially implemented as single-choice
  • WhatsApp, Telegram and copy-link sharing
  • Responder-link generator and source/campaign tagging

What we learned: the mechanics worked, but the educational depth, trust content, RAHO story and visual presentation were too shallow. The final intent step also needed to allow multiple selections.
