FlowFirst Demand Acquisition

CPQL Agency Pilot Implementation Plan

A practical 30-day plan for testing external performance-acquisition partners for Cambodia stroke recovery — without giving away FlowFirst's internal CAC, margins or total marketing budget.

Acquisition Phase 1Performance-Based AcquisitionCambodia

Core operating principle

FlowFirst owns the funnel. Partners fill the funnel. We buy defined outcomes, verify them ourselves, and scale only the partners that create real downstream value.

Default ownership boundary: the partner controls Audience → Response. FlowFirst controls Enquiry → Qualification → Assessment → Recommended Pathway → Customer. A partner may handle deeper qualification only where rules, consent, transparency and verification are strong enough.

1. Pilot objective

Goal: identify 2–3 acquisition partners capable of consistently generating genuine stroke-recovery families in Cambodia on a measurable performance basis.

Initial market: stroke patients who are medically stable or discharged, their spouse/adult children/caregivers, and families actively looking for further recovery options.

Offer promoted: a FlowFirst stroke-recovery conversation / assessment — not a promise of cure and not an emergency stroke service.

2. Recommended commercial ladder

Level A — Valid Enquiry

Real person, valid contact, stroke-related, consented, nonduplicate, attributable to the partner.

Best starting point when FlowFirst retains qualification control.

Level B — Qualified Lead

Meets the valid-enquiry rules plus agreed recovery, decision-maker and access criteria.

Use when qualification rules are objective and disputes can be resolved from evidence.

Level C — Attended Assessment

The prospect actually attends a FlowFirst phone/video/in-person assessment.

Strong performance model for mature partners that manage follow-up and booking.

Internal only: FlowFirst should still calculate cost per valid enquiry, CPQL, cost per assessment and CAC for every source. Partners do not need to know FlowFirst's customer revenue, gross margin, CAC ceiling or maximum willingness to pay.

3. Billable lead definition

  • Unique individual and not already in the FlowFirst database within the agreed lookback period.
  • Patient has had a stroke, or the enquiry is from a close family decision-maker/caregiver for a stroke patient.
  • Valid phone / WhatsApp / Telegram contact information.
  • Explicit consent for FlowFirst to contact them.
  • Not an acute emergency case requiring immediate emergency medical care.
  • Actively interested in further recovery, rehabilitation or assessment.
  • Potentially able to access private services in Cambodia.
  • Source is attributable to the partner through a unique landing page, QR code or campaign source code.

Reject: duplicates, fake contacts, bots, scraped lists without consent, unrelated enquiries, emergency stroke cases, people who deny submitting the enquiry, or leads generated using misleading medical claims.

5. The 30-day pilot

Days 1–7 — Infrastructure

  • Stroke landing page
  • Short enquiry form
  • Consent wording
  • CRM fields
  • Duplicate detection
  • Partner source codes
  • Acceptance/rejection workflow

Days 8–14 — Procurement

  • Send partner brief to 8–12 suppliers
  • Ask each to quote outcome and price
  • Do not disclose CAC ceiling
  • Review method, claims, geography and data handling
  • Select 3–5 pilot partners

Days 15–30 — Live Pilot

  • Issue unique URLs / QR codes
  • Receive leads directly into FlowFirst
  • Verify within agreed SLA
  • Track downstream qualification and assessment
  • Weekly partner quality review

6. What every partner receives

  • One-page FlowFirst Demand Acquisition Partner Brief.
  • Target-audience definition.
  • Billable-lead definition and rejection rules.
  • Approved claims / prohibited claims guide.
  • Partner-specific URL, QR code and source code.
  • FlowFirst contact destination and handoff process.
  • Acceptance / rejection SLA and payment cadence.
  • Simple partner dashboard: Submitted → Pending → Accepted → Rejected → Payable → Paid.

7. What FlowFirst keeps internal

Partner-facing

  • Audience
  • Offer
  • Lead definition
  • Claims rules
  • Tracking rules
  • Commercial price agreed with that partner

FlowFirst-only

  • CAC ceiling
  • Customer revenue / LTV
  • Gross margin
  • Total acquisition budget
  • Channel budget allocations
  • Scale / kill thresholds
  • Downstream economics across other partners

9. Partner decision rules after 30 days

Scale

High acceptance rate, strong downstream conversion, reliable consent, transparent reporting, compliant messaging.

Continue / Modify

Promising audience or economics but needs better targeting, qualification or creative.

Stop

Low-quality or disputed leads, poor contactability, misleading claims, duplicate/fraud problems, or economics that do not improve.

10. Outreach brief

Suggested opening:

FlowFirst is establishing a performance-based patient acquisition pilot in Cambodia focused initially on stroke recovery. We are looking for partners who can generate genuine, consented stroke-patient or family enquiries on a measurable outcome basis. We prefer partners to propose their own acquisition method and quote their price per agreed outcome. FlowFirst will provide the landing page, tracking, lead criteria and compliance boundaries, and will independently verify every submitted lead.

Ask each supplier to answer:

  1. Which outcome are you prepared to price against: valid enquiry, qualified lead, booked assessment, or attended assessment?
  2. What is your price per outcome?
  3. Who funds media?
  4. What channels would you use in Cambodia?
  5. How will consent and lead authenticity be verified?
  6. What information will accompany every lead?
  7. Can you work under healthcare advertising and claims restrictions?
  8. What minimum volume or commitment do you require?

Immediate action list

  1. Finalize the stroke enquiry landing page and CRM acceptance fields.
  2. Finalize the one-page external partner brief.
  3. Contact the six named candidates plus 4–6 additional local/regional suppliers.
  4. Collect quotes without revealing FlowFirst's internal CAC ceiling or total budget.
  5. Select 3–5 partners with meaningfully different acquisition methods.
  6. Run the first 30-day cohort under unique source codes.
  7. Rank on downstream economics, not headline lead price.

Operating shorthand: Buy outcomes first → build strategic channels → fund what proves itself → own what becomes core.
