AFW Holdings × La Vista One Club Independent Partner Network

A reciprocal business development proposal centred on helping Angie Chew sell more La Vista One condominiums, while creating additional business for AFW Sanctuary, AFW Lifestyle Village and participating independent partners.

For discussion · Not yet an agreement

Prepared as an independent collaboration proposal. No affiliation, endorsement or developer authorization is implied.

1. Executive Summary

The first test of success is straightforward: does this alliance help Angie generate more qualified La Vista One buyers and complete more condominium sales? The proposal begins with that priority—not with a request for Angie to sell AFW products.

La Vista One Club's independent participants bring property-sales relationships, design expertise, tourism services and local business networks. AFW brings a working countryside wellness destination, accommodation, a lifestyle-property concept and connections with Cambodian and international audiences. By combining these complementary capabilities, participants can create new reasons for prospective buyers to visit, engage and purchase.

Proposed positioning: Your city home, your countryside escape, and a connected lifestyle community. La Vista One is the featured urban condominium in Angie-led initiatives; AFW Sanctuary is the countryside wellness destination. Each partner remains an independent business.

Proposed arrangement: one non-exclusive alliance framework, individual project agreements, a 90-day measurable pilot, and no upfront joint-venture commitments.

2. Angie's Condo Sales: The Primary Commercial Objective

01 · BUYER DISCOVERY

International buyer introductions

Identify suitable purchasers through AFW's Singapore, Malaysia, Cambodia and broader professional relationships. Angie qualifies prospects, verifies eligibility, conducts viewings and manages the sale.

02 · BUYER VISITS

Phnom Penh property discovery visits

Invite genuine property prospects to scheduled La Vista viewings, with optional AFW countryside hospitality before or after the city programme. State clearly that the visit includes property marketing.

03 · BUYER BENEFITS

Condo buyer wellness privileges

Offer clearly costed AFW Sanctuary retreat discounts or limited stay vouchers to eligible condo purchasers, subject to written funding, availability and fulfilment terms.

04 · SALES EVENTS

Joint city-lifestyle events

Host property investment information evenings, lifestyle showcases and small-group buyer sessions in Phnom Penh or Singapore. Separate verified property facts from promotional lifestyle messages.

05 · REPEAT ENGAGEMENT

Resident and referral community

Give existing condo owners optional wellness experiences, community visits and reasons to invite friends—creating opportunities for authentic buyer referrals, not guaranteed sales.

Before marketing, confirm Angie's available units, current pricing, inventory control, developer permissions, commissions and buyer eligibility. Do not advertise yields, guaranteed appreciation or financing without verified written support.

4. Commercial Model — Principles to Negotiate

The alliance should be non-exclusive and transaction-based. Independent partners keep their own customers, staff, legal entities, accounts and operational responsibilities. For every campaign or referral stream, the partners sign a short written schedule covering the lead source, marketing expenditure, responsibilities, commission calculation and payment conditions.

5. Practical Joint Campaigns

CAMPAIGN A

Discover Phnom Penh + Escape to Nature

A property-centred itinerary: buyer briefing, La Vista One viewing, neighborhood discovery, and an optional AFW Sanctuary visit. Angie is the named sales lead. Track attended viewings and offers.

CAMPAIGN B

La Vista One Buyer Privileges

A modest AFW wellness voucher or preferred retreat package as an optional benefit for confirmed qualifying purchasers. Define sponsor, face value, redemption period and maximum quantity.

CAMPAIGN C

City Home + Countryside Cottage

Separate, clearly priced property propositions offered only when the prospect is genuinely interested. Neither purchase is conditional on the other.

CAMPAIGN D

Friends of La Vista Lifestyle Evening

Small invitation-only gathering featuring Angie's verified condo inventory, AFW's countryside experience and selected independent partners. Attendees opt in separately to follow-up by each partner.

Suggested scorecard

Primary: Angie-qualified condo prospects, viewings attended, offers received, reservations and completed La Vista condo sales, plus marketing cost per completed sale.

Secondary: AFW paid guest bookings, Lifestyle Village qualified enquiries, cottage design contracts, tour bookings, revenue and commissions actually collected.

Operating discipline: Track every enquiry with a campaign code, original source, consent and assigned follow-up owner. Share only the minimum necessary personal details and honor customers' communication preferences.

7. Partner Roles and Governance

Angie Chew: Lead the condo-sales objective, define property inventory and prospects, oversee approved property-facing promotions, and convene suitable independent La Vista contacts.

AFW Holdings / Sanctuary: Lead Sanctuary experience design, approved wellness offers, lifestyle-property information, suitable international introductions and on-site partner hospitality.

ANI Studio / At enn Neoh: Assess a competitively costed tropical cottage design-and-build proposal; scope and fees require a separate engagement.

Chea Taypheng: Explore legally compliant travel itineraries and visitor logistics on agreed commercial rates.

Other La Vista participants: Opt into opportunities relevant to their services; participation is not presumed.

Governance: Two coordinators (Angie and AFW nominee), a brief weekly pilot review, monthly commercial scorecard, joint approval of branded materials, a written non-circumvention/lead-credit process if needed, and an escalation path for customer issues. No party may bind another or represent an unauthorized affiliation with the La Vista developer.

8. Decisions for the First Workshop

  • Which La Vista units can Angie currently sell, at what verified prices and commissions?
  • What buyer groups and countries does Angie most want to reach over the next 90 days?
  • Which two campaigns can the partners execute with existing resources?
  • What exact referral fees, allowable marketing costs and payment triggers are commercially acceptable and lawful?
  • Can authorized parties approve co-branded materials, property photography, project naming and buyer privileges?
  • Who owns follow-up, consent handling, customer service and performance reporting?
  • Which independent team members are ready to participate immediately?

Proposed first joint commitment

Agree to a 90-day non-exclusive pilot, with additional La Vista One condominium sales as the lead success measure; launch one condo-buyer acquisition campaign and one reciprocal AFW lifestyle offer; review actual results before any deeper commercial integration.

9. Invitation to Collaborate

We see genuine value in linking an urban residential community with an operating countryside wellness sanctuary and a network of independent specialists. The opportunity is not to merge companies or create another complicated structure. It is to help each participant win more business through trusted introductions, useful experiences and professional delivery.

Angie's success is central: more credible buyer interest, more attended viewings and more completed La Vista One condominium sales. A partnership that achieves this, while also creating measurable income for AFW and the participating professionals, will have earned the right to grow.
