Partnership Proposal — 29 September 2026

PhysioCam × AFW Sanctuary / FlowFirst

A two-pronged partnership for stroke recovery: PhysioCam extends into AFW, while FlowFirst may progressively extend into PhysioCam.

The Opportunity

A partnership that works in both directions

We believe PhysioCam and AFW Sanctuary can create a partnership that is broader than simply supplying therapists to AFW.

PhysioCam extends into AFW.
FlowFirst may extend into PhysioCam.

The first opportunity is to jointly operate a residential stroke recovery program at AFW Sanctuary. The second is to explore how FlowFirst / NOVO-supported services may, where appropriate and permitted, be introduced into PhysioCam’s existing centres.

Prong 1

PhysioCam extends into AFW

PhysioCam bringsRehabilitation assessment, physiotherapy, gait and mobility training, occupational and functional rehabilitation, therapist supervision, rehabilitation protocols and outcome measurement.
AFW bringsPrivate accommodation, nursing support, meals, hospitality, gym and wellness facilities, recovery therapies, patient coordination and residential operations.

The result is a dedicated residential stroke recovery program without PhysioCam having to build and operate its own residential facility.

PhysioCam does not need to build a residential facility. AFW does not need to become a rehabilitation company.
Prong 2

FlowFirst may extend into PhysioCam

The partnership should not stop at AFW. As PhysioCam becomes familiar with the FlowFirst approach and NOVO through the residential program, we would also like to explore whether selected FlowFirst-supported services can eventually be offered within PhysioCam’s own centres.

This may allow PhysioCam to broaden its offering beyond conventional rehabilitation and create an additional service and revenue stream.

At AFWPhysioCam rehabilitation expertise becomes part of the residential recovery program.
At PhysioCamFlowFirst-supported services may progressively become part of the recovery offering.

Any NOVO-related service would be introduced only under an appropriate medical, operational and regulatory framework.

Why It Matters

What PhysioCam potentially gains

Residential rehabilitationA new service category without major property investment.
Additional revenueRevenue from residential rehabilitation plus potential future FlowFirst-supported services.
Longer patient relationshipsA two-way pathway between outpatient and residential rehabilitation.
DifferentiationA broader recovery offering than conventional physiotherapy alone.
Rehabilitation remains the foundation. FlowFirst is intended to support the recovery environment around it.

Pilot

Start with the first 3 patients

We suggest beginning with a small pilot and using the first cases to jointly determine:

Which patients are suitable
How much therapy is required
Staffing and equipment needs
Pricing and revenue sharing
Outcome measurements
Operational workflow

The objective is to prove the model clinically and commercially before scaling it.
