# Think Flow First — Development History

This file records major working versions of ThinkFlowFirst.com for review, stakeholder education, and product-development learning.

## V1 — Functional Prototype
**Date:** 6 Oct 2026  
**Frozen branch:** `history/v1-functional-prototype`  
**Live archive:** https://thinkflowfirst-v1-history.onrender.com

### Purpose
Prove the responder journey before investing in deeper content, visual design, multilingual support, voice input, and production integrations.

### Included
- Topic-specific entry doors
- Basic Flow First explanation
- Basic RAHO credibility layer
- Seven-question intake
- Contact capture
- Three follow-up intentions
- Sharing
- Campaign/source tracking
- Internal responder link generator

### Review findings
- Functionality worked.
- Flow First explanation was too lean.
- RAHO page was too shallow.
- About/Trust and Contact content were missing or insufficient.
- Visual treatment was too weak.
- Follow-up intentions should be multi-select, not mutually exclusive.
- Lead submission was captured in the staging server log but not yet routed to a CRM/advisor.
- Multilingual and voice response should follow shortly after the English journey is stabilized.
