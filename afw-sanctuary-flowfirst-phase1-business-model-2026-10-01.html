AFW Sanctuary Phase 1
FlowFirst Wellness Business Model

A practical proposal to turn AFW Sanctuary's existing assets into a structured residential wellness business built around the FlowFirst philosophy, with Advanced Flow / NOVO as a visible premium pathway.

Prepared for AFW stakeholders · Phase 1 commercial and operating framework
Executive summary

The proposition

AFW Sanctuary already has most of the infrastructure required to operate a differentiated residential wellness business. Phase 1 does not require AFW to become a hospital, an intensive rehabilitation centre, or a high-cost Western-style longevity clinic.

The opportunity is to stop selling a collection of therapies and start selling structured FlowFirst residential programs.

The core philosophy is simple: movement, circulation, breathing, hydration, nutrition, sleep, recovery and appropriate technologies all support better flow and better function.

NOVO should not be hidden until a guest arrives. It should be visible during the sales process as Advanced Flow, while remaining optional and clinically appropriate. The residential stay can also become the onboarding point for guests who later continue selected services at home.

04 · What is deferred

Stroke Recovery moves to Phase 2

Stroke remains commercially attractive, but full stroke recovery is a different operating model. Dependent stroke guests can require 1:1 caregivers, physiotherapy, transfer support, fall-risk management, greater nursing involvement, higher clinical oversight and more complex liability management.

Decision: Phase 1 will focus on independent wellness guests. Formal Stroke Recovery & Rehabilitation will be developed as a Phase 2 specialist program after the rehab equipment, physiotherapy team, caregiver model and clinical protocols are ready.

06 · Advanced Flow

NOVO is visible from the first sales conversation

The commercial architecture has two levels:

FlowFirst Residential Core program built around movement, circulation, nutrition, recovery and the AFW environment.

FlowFirst Advanced Core residential program plus NOVO where clinically appropriate.

For planning, NOVO is priced at US$150 per infusion with US$38 COGS per infusion. The Phase 1 model assumes that an Advanced Flow guest may receive one infusion for each occupied night: 4D3N = 3 infusions; 7D6N = 6 infusions.

Important commercial rule: NOVO is non-commissionable. It is purchased directly from AFW and paid on site. Future home-service NOVO is also outside the residential referral commission unless separately agreed.

13 · Facility economics

Separate US$6,000 monthly facility rental charge

The Sanctuary itself has an economic cost and should not be treated as free simply because AFW owns the asset. The FlowFirst operating business therefore pays a planning facility rental / capital charge of US$6,000 per month.

This charge represents use of the seven suites, wellness/medical areas, kitchen, pool, gym, grounds, infrastructure and other property assets, and is intended to support the economic burden of AFW's property financing.

Operating/fixed cost$16,000 per month
Facility rental$6,000 per month
Property-adjusted fixed base$22,000 per month

For conventional accounting, loan principal repayment is not a P&L operating expense. The facility rental charge is used here as a management/economic allocation so the operating business carries the cost of using the Sanctuary asset.

16 · Break-even

Break-even moves with Advanced Flow penetration

At 40% NOVO penetration and the conservative 70% referral-channel assumption, contribution after variable costs and referral commissions is approximately $243.60 per occupied room-night. Covering the US$22,000 monthly fixed + facility base therefore requires about 90 occupied room-nights, or approximately 43% occupancy.

At 60% NOVO penetration, contribution rises to approximately $266.00 per occupied room-night, lowering break-even to roughly 39–40% occupancy.

19 · Phase 1 decisions

Recommended stakeholder approval

  1. Launch the five Phase 1 programs: Reset, Longevity, Executive Reset, Couples Reset and Active Aging.
  2. Defer formal Stroke Recovery & Rehabilitation to Phase 2.
  3. Set the public FlowFirst residential program rate at US$330 per occupied night.
  4. Price NOVO at US$150 per infusion, non-commissionable and paid directly to AFW.
  5. Allow residential referral commission from 10% to a maximum of 20% depending on partner contribution.
  6. Recruit immediately: Wellness Director, Program Coordinator and Movement & Wellness Coach.
  7. Approve approximately US$17,000 for Phase 1 China-direct equipment and setup.
  8. Use US$16,000/month as the operating/fixed planning base plus US$6,000/month facility rental.
  9. Manage toward 50%+ occupancy initially and 70% occupancy as the operating target.
  10. Build Advanced Flow into the initial sales proposition rather than waiting until arrival.
20 · Conclusion

A lean business built around assets AFW already owns

AFW does not need another major construction project before it can launch a coherent new wellness business. The immediate opportunity is to organize existing infrastructure, staff and therapies into a clear residential product and strengthen it with better program management, modest China-direct procurement and disciplined customer acquisition.

Fill the rooms with FlowFirst. Increase guest value with Advanced Flow. Extend the relationship through continued care.

At the target operating level of 70% occupancy and 40–60% Advanced Flow penetration, the model produces approximately US$688k–741k annual revenue and US$166k–205k annual property-adjusted operating profit under a conservative assumption that 70% of bookings carry a 20% residential referral commission.

All financial figures are management planning estimates and should be reconciled against actual AFW payroll, operating costs, tax treatment, clinical requirements and realized guest mix before final investment approval.
