Strategic Partner Collaboration & Commercial Validation

Global demand creation & commercialization strategic partner briefing.

What we ask the partner

Critically audit our strategic work and challenge the underlying assumptions. We do not want only a content-production agency. Assess creative, conversion, distribution, localization, economics and compliance with independent judgment.

Specific decisions

Is DM-first optimal versus a policy-compliant landing page? Which hook families generate qualified rather than superficial engagement? Should influencers, clinicians, search, communities or ads lead? Which global corridor offers strongest feasible contribution? What system should be built now and what can wait?

Four phases

1. Independent audit and redesign: diagnose existing library, creative, claims, website, channels, CRM and ability to measure purchases.
2. Controlled market pilot: choose lawful source market, one or two audiences, contrasting hook families and conversion paths, pre-agreed media spend.
3. Unit economics: qualified response rates, consult attendance, CAC, revenue, contribution, follow-on outcomes and user research.
4. Strategic partnership: negotiate scopes, geography, governance, IP, data, performance terms and potentially equity only after due diligence.

Pilot brief template

Market: to be jointly selected. Audience: stroke-related educational questions or longevity. Channels: at least two meaningful alternatives where practical. Duration: 30–90 days illustrative. Budgets require approval and should not assume grants. All provider promotions require legal clearance.

Partnership terms to settle

Ownership of creative assets and software; confidentiality; consented data transfer; creator attribution; clinical responsibility; reporting access; limitations on disease claims; conflict of interest; approved budget authority; termination, handover and audit rights.

Success definition

A credible, compliant and measurable system capable of converting qualified consumer interest to appropriate lawful services or future-access lists, with validated economics before scaling internationally.
