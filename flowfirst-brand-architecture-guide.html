Master Brand

1. FlowFirst™ is who we are

FlowFirst™ is the global master brand and platform. Country teams should not create separate master brands unless a compelling regulatory reason requires it.

Use one word with two capital Fs: FlowFirst.

Examples: FlowFirst Malaysia, FlowFirst Cambodia, FlowFirst Singapore, FlowFirst Recovery, FlowFirst Connect, FlowFirst NOVO.

Campaign / Education

2. Think Flow First. is what we say

Think Flow First. stays as three words because it is a sentence and a call to action. It is not another company or master brand.

Its job is education, awareness, disease-specific campaigns, social content, demand generation and helping people ask better questions.

FlowFirst is the brand. Think Flow First is the idea.

Examples: “Think Flow First. Before and after stroke.” · “Trillions of cells. One circulation. Think Flow First.”

Decision

3. FlowCare is absorbed, not promoted as a separate brand

We concluded that FlowCare creates unnecessary public complexity beside Think Flow First and FlowFirst.

Education, public guidance and “what happens next?” contentMove under Think Flow First.
Navigation, referral, coordination and follow-upMove under FlowFirst Connect.

Useful FlowCare concepts can survive as campaigns. For example: “They told you what’s wrong. Nobody told you what happens next.” can be a Think Flow First campaign line without keeping FlowCare as a separate identity.

Service Name

4. FlowFirst Connect

FlowFirst Connect is the single named navigation and referral service. It replaces both FlowFirst Refer and the separate FlowCare brand concept.

FlowFirst Connect helps you understand your next step and connects you with the right service, provider or pathway.

It can cover referral, navigation, coordination, follow-up and family support, and may connect users to FlowFirst centers, partner clinics, rehabilitation providers, specialists, diagnostics, hospitals or other appropriate providers.

FlowFirst Connect is a service under FlowFirst — not a separate master brand.
Physical Format

5. FlowFirst Center for Recovery & Longevity

FLOWFIRST
Center for Recovery & Longevity

Johor Bahru

Recovery. Prevention. Longevity.

From Recovery to Longevity.

The word Center is intentional. It should feel institutional and consultation-led — not like an IV shop, spa, aesthetics clinic or full hospital.

For the JB center, which is about 120 sqm with a front doctor/advisor consultation room and around eight infusion recliners, the center does not need to deliver every recovery or longevity service itself. It can consult, assess, provide selected interventions, follow up and refer.

Consultation first. Appropriate intervention second.

6. Program families

FlowFirst LongevityLongevity and health-optimization program family.

Stroke: two wedges

RecoveryFor the survivor: “What can we do now?”
PreventionFor family and friends: “Could this happen to me?”
One stroke event creates two conversations: recovery for the survivor, prevention for the people around them.
Treatment & Technology

7. NOVO and IGDS

FlowFirst NOVOPatient-facing treatment / infusion name. This is what consumers should remember.

FlowFirst NOVO — using IGDS technology.
Partner Designations

8. How partner outlets should be branded

FlowFirst CenterFull FlowFirst-branded center operating to full center standards.
FlowFirst Partner CenterIndependent operator approved to offer defined FlowFirst programs or services.
Authorized FlowFirst ProviderDoctor, clinic or smaller participating site offering selected FlowFirst services.
Powered by FlowFirstEndorsement mark for a host center that keeps its own established brand.

Examples: ABC Medical Centre — FlowFirst Partner Center · XYZ Longevity Clinic — Powered by FlowFirst.

Origin Story

9. RAHO Club

RAHO Club remains important as part of the Indonesian origin, practical operating experience and early real-world learning.

RAHO is where much of the story began. FlowFirst is how that story travels internationally.

RAHO should not become the international master consumer brand.

Regional Efficiency

12. Why one system matters

One campaign built in Cambodia can be adapted for Malaysia. One stroke funnel built in Malaysia can be localized for Singapore. One investor deck can explain the same architecture globally. One body of content strengthens the same brand.

Create once. Localize many times.

Every marketing dollar should ideally build FlowFirst brand equity rather than a separate country identity.
