Master Brand

1. FlowFirst™ is the master ecosystem

FlowFirst™ is the global brand and operating philosophy. It connects education, navigation, physical locations, partner providers, programs and technologies into one system.

Use one word with two capital Fs: FlowFirst.

FlowFirst is the system. Everything else explains how people discover it, access it, experience it or deliver it.
Education & Demand

2. ThinkFlowFirst is the public movement

ThinkFlowFirst is the education and awareness platform. It creates curiosity, explains the importance of circulation and flow, builds disease-specific campaigns, and generates demand.

It is where hooks, social content, public education, community funnels and awareness campaigns live.

ThinkFlowFirst makes people ask a better question before they decide what to do next.

Examples include: “Before you travel overseas for treatment, Think Flow First.” and “Trillions of cells. One circulation. Think Flow First.”

Navigation & Human Help

3. FlowCare helps people work out what to do next

FlowCare should remain as the patient and family navigation layer. Its original purpose is still valuable: helping people after a diagnosis, health concern or major event when they are confused about their next step.

FlowCare can explain options, coordinate referrals, help families compare pathways, connect people with suitable providers and follow up afterwards. It is not positioned as a clinic.

They told you what’s wrong. Nobody told you what happens next.
FlowCare = “We help you figure out what to do next.”

FlowCare may refer a person to a FlowClub, AFW Sanctuary, a hospital, rehabilitation provider, physician, overseas provider or another FlowFirst Certified Partner. The recommendation should follow the person’s needs, not force every case into a FlowFirst-owned location.

Current direction: FlowCare replaces the need for a separate FlowFirst Connect brand. The navigation role should not be duplicated under two names.

Selected Physical Locations

4. FlowClub is where people experience FlowFirst

FlowClub is the preferred consumer-facing name for selected branded physical locations. It is stronger and more distinctive than the generic term “FlowFirst Center.”

A FlowClub is not simply an IV outlet and should not feel like a conventional clinic with a new name. It can combine prevention, recovery, longevity, biohacking, assessments, selected therapies, education and community.

FlowClub. Experience FlowFirst.

The brand narrative is simple: most healthcare begins when something is already wrong. FlowClub can begin earlier — helping people stay well, recover better and age with greater confidence.

Healthy / PreventiveFor people who want to understand risk, stay well and act earlier.
Longevity / PerformanceFor people who want better function, resilience, energy and healthspan.
RecoveryFor people rebuilding function after illness, injury or a major health event.
CommunityFor recurring education, membership, progress tracking and ongoing engagement.
FlowClub should be selective. Not every partner clinic becomes a FlowClub.
Network Expansion

5. Independent clinics remain independent

We should not use the FlowClub name everywhere. Independent clinics and centers should generally keep their own identity and use a FlowFirst relationship designation.

FlowFirst Certified PartnerPreferred broad designation for an independent clinic or center that meets defined FlowFirst standards.
FlowFirst Recovery PartnerFor rehabilitation or recovery-focused partners.
FlowFirst ProviderUseful for doctors or smaller clinical providers offering approved services.
Powered by FlowFirstOptional endorsement for established host brands where a softer relationship mark is more appropriate.

Example: PhysioCam — FlowFirst Recovery Partner or ABC Medical Centre — FlowFirst Certified Partner.

FlowClub is a branded destination. FlowFirst Certified Partner is a network designation.

Technology, Therapy & Local Brands

7. Keep the technology, therapy and commercial brand layers distinct

The public-facing ThinkFlowFirst language should stay simple and consistent: IGDS is the underlying RAHO technology platform, while Advanced Flow is the patient-facing therapy based on that technology.

IGDSUnderlying RAHO technology platform. Use for scientific, professional, research, regulatory, evidence and origin-story contexts.
Advanced FlowThe standard patient-facing therapy name used by ThinkFlowFirst and in public education.
NOVOMay be used outside ThinkFlowFirst as an approved country-specific, operator-specific or white-label commercial name.
VasqClearMay be used as an approved commercial or white-label brand where the market architecture calls for it; it should not be presented as a separate underlying technology if it is based on IGDS.
ThinkFlowFirst standard: IGDS = technology. Advanced Flow = therapy. NOVO, VasqClear and future approved names = optional commercial / country-specific / white-label branding.

This allows the science and education story to remain globally consistent while giving countries, operators and partners flexibility in commercial branding.

Preferred public wording: “Advanced Flow is an intravenous therapy based on RAHO’s IGDS nanobubble technology.” After that first explanation, use Advanced Flow for the therapy.

Origin Story

8. RAHO Club remains part of the origin story

RAHO Club remains important as part of the Indonesian operating history, real-world experience and development story behind the technology.

RAHO is where much of the story began. FlowFirst is how the story can travel internationally.

RAHO should not become the international master consumer brand.

Advanced Flow or other appropriate services

This is a brand architecture, not a mandatory clinical sequence. Not every person needs every layer.
