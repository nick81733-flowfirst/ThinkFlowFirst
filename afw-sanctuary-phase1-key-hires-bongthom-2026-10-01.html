AFW Sanctuary Phase 1 — Key Hires

BongThom-ready recruitment copy for the three priority hires required to launch AFW Sanctuary’s Phase 1 FlowFirst residential wellness business.

AFW Sanctuary · Kampong Speu · Cambodia

Phase 1 recruitment priority

AFW Sanctuary is launching structured FlowFirst residential wellness programs focused on movement, circulation, healthy aging, recovery, nutrition, sleep and guest wellbeing.

1. Wellness DirectorBuild and lead the wellness business.
2. Program CoordinatorOwn the daily guest schedule and coordination.
3. Movement & Wellness CoachLead safe daily movement, mobility and wellness activities.

These are recruitment-oriented versions for public posting. More detailed internal job descriptions can be used during shortlisting and interviews.

Priority Hire 1

Wellness Director — FlowFirst Programs

Location: AFW Sanctuary, Kampong SpeuSalary: USD 1,500–2,000/month + performance incentiveEmployment: Full-time

AFW Sanctuary is building a new range of residential wellness programs focused on movement, circulation, healthy aging, recovery and better daily function.

We are looking for a hands-on Wellness Director to help build and lead this business.

This is not a normal spa-manager position. You will be responsible for the entire wellness guest experience — from program design and daily scheduling to staff coordination, service quality and business growth.

What you will do

  • Lead our FlowFirst residential wellness programs.
  • Coordinate nurses, therapists, movement coaches, kitchen and guest services.
  • Create smooth daily schedules and a consistent guest journey.
  • Develop SOPs, checklists and program standards.
  • Train staff and improve service quality.
  • Monitor guest satisfaction, progress and outcomes.
  • Help improve packages, pricing, referrals and repeat business.
  • Work closely with management to grow the wellness business.

We are looking for someone who

  • Has 3–5+ years of experience in wellness, hospitality, healthcare operations, spa management or a similar field.
  • Has management or supervisory experience.
  • Speaks good English; Khmer is strongly preferred.
  • Is organized, practical and good with people.
  • Can build systems, not just follow them.
  • Understands high-quality guest service.
  • Is commercially minded and comfortable working with health and wellness professionals.
  • Is willing to work at AFW Sanctuary in Kampong Speu.

Strong advantage: experience in wellness resorts, longevity centers, destination spas, medical wellness, rehabilitation, health retreats or international hospitality.

Who will do well here: someone who enjoys building something new, solving problems, working with a small team and taking ownership. If you want a role where you can help create a new wellness business from the ground up, we would like to hear from you.
Priority Hire 2

Program Coordinator — FlowFirst Programs

Location: AFW Sanctuary, Kampong SpeuSalary: USD 500–700/monthEmployment: Full-time

AFW Sanctuary is looking for a highly organized and friendly Program Coordinator to help manage our new residential wellness programs.

You will be the person who makes sure every guest knows what they are doing, every staff member knows what is happening, and the daily program runs smoothly.

What you will do

  • Prepare and manage daily guest schedules.
  • Coordinate wellness activities, massage, EECP, PEMF, meals and movement sessions.
  • Communicate with guests throughout their stay.
  • Coordinate nurses, therapists, kitchen, housekeeping and other departments.
  • Maintain guest records, schedules and checklists.
  • Assist with guest arrivals, orientation and departures.
  • Handle guest requests and solve scheduling problems.
  • Collect feedback and help improve the guest experience.
  • Support repeat bookings, referrals and program upgrades.

We are looking for someone who

  • Has experience in hospitality, wellness, clinics, guest relations or administration.
  • Speaks good English and Khmer.
  • Is very organized and dependable.
  • Is friendly and professional with guests.
  • Can manage many small details at the same time.
  • Is comfortable using spreadsheets, messaging apps and scheduling tools.
  • Takes responsibility and follows through.
  • Is willing to work at AFW Sanctuary in Kampong Speu.

Strong advantage: hotel guest-relations, clinic-coordinator, spa or wellness experience.

A good fit: someone who enjoys organizing people, helping guests and making sure every part of the day runs properly.
Priority Hire 3

Movement & Wellness Coach

Location: AFW Sanctuary, Kampong SpeuSalary: USD 450–650/monthEmployment: Full-time

AFW Sanctuary is looking for an energetic and caring Movement & Wellness Coach to work with guests in our residential wellness programs.

Most guests will be middle-aged or older adults who want to improve mobility, strength, fitness, energy and overall wellbeing.

This is not a bodybuilding or high-intensity fitness role.

What you will do

  • Lead daily guided walking sessions.
  • Lead stretching and mobility exercises.
  • Conduct simple strength and resistance exercises.
  • Lead balance and coordination activities.
  • Support pool-based movement activities where appropriate.
  • Lead simple breathing and relaxation sessions.
  • Encourage guests to become more active safely.
  • Help conduct simple functional assessments such as walking speed, sit-to-stand, basic balance, walking distance and grip strength.
  • Work closely with nurses and the wellness team.
  • Adjust activities according to age and ability.

We are looking for someone who

  • Has experience in fitness, sports coaching, wellness, physiotherapy assistance or personal training.
  • Enjoys working with middle-aged and older adults.
  • Is patient, encouraging and professional.
  • Understands safe exercise and movement.
  • Speaks Khmer; basic English is required.
  • Can demonstrate exercises clearly.
  • Is physically fit and reliable.
  • Is willing to work at AFW Sanctuary in Kampong Speu.

Strong advantage: experience in senior fitness, mobility training, yoga, Pilates, physiotherapy assistance or rehabilitation exercise; CPR or first-aid certification is also useful.

Important: the Movement & Wellness Coach does not diagnose or treat medical conditions. Guests with medical concerns are referred to the nursing or medical team.

Recruitment sequence

AFW should ideally recruit the Wellness Director first. The successful Wellness Director should then participate in interviewing and selecting the Program Coordinator and Movement & Wellness Coach, helping ensure the Phase 1 team works as one integrated unit.
