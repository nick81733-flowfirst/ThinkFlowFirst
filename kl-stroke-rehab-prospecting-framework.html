Purpose & ground rules

Identify stroke rehabilitation centres, neurological physiotherapists, residential recovery operators, infusion clinics, mobile IV/nursing services, NGOs and hospitals for introductory conversations. The first objective is relationship discovery, not patient recruitment or any claim that Advanced Flow treats stroke.

Prospect list, not approved clinical partners. Verify identity, phone, operating location and email before outreach. “Verified” means a contact appeared on an official published page, not that they agreed to collaborate. WhatsApp shortcuts open a chat only if that number is WhatsApp-enabled. Mobile contact does not guarantee WhatsApp.

International dialing: Malaysian domestic 0 is removed after +60. Example: 012-906 1690 → +60 12-906 1690; WhatsApp URL uses 60129061690.

Team prospecting approach

  1. Day 1: validate contact details; identify owner, clinical director or rehabilitation lead.
  2. Day 2–3: send a short non-disease-claim introduction and request a meeting.
  3. Meeting: establish stroke caseload, rehabilitation stages, existing physician links, patient education needs and appetite for evidence-based collaboration.
  4. Follow-up: share technical background and evidence transparently, then refer any infusion suitability questions to qualified Malaysian clinicians and regulatory advisers.

Meeting outcome fields: assigned agent · date contacted · replied? · decision-maker · appointment date · next action · objections · evidence requested · compliance review.

WhatsApp introductions — copy as short paragraphs

Hi, good morning.

We are from Flow First and are exploring clinical partnerships in Kuala Lumpur for an emerging infusion-based technology called Advanced Flow.

We understand that your team provides IV or nursing services, and we would like to discuss whether there may be a suitable clinical collaboration.

Would your clinic owner, doctor or clinical manager be available for a short introductory meeting?

For stroke rehabilitation and recovery organizations

Hi, good morning.

We are from Flow First and are exploring collaborations with stroke rehabilitation and recovery organizations in KL.

We would appreciate an introductory meeting with your management or clinical team to share the background and available evidence for an emerging infusion-based technology called Advanced Flow.

Our aim is to explore whether there may be any appropriate opportunities for education or collaboration, subject to independent medical and regulatory review.

Would your team be open to a short discussion?

Meeting qualification questions

  1. How many active stroke-recovery patients do you support monthly?
  2. Do you have a rehabilitation physician or medical director?
  3. How do you currently coordinate neurologist referrals and community education?
  4. Do you collaborate with external providers? What consent and conflict-of-interest rules apply?
  5. Who would review emerging evidence and regulatory status before discussing any adjunct therapy?

Compliance & clinical boundary

Stroke recovery depends on timely evidence-based acute care, rehabilitation and secondary prevention. Advanced Flow/IGDS is not established here as a proven stroke treatment. No outcome promises, unsolicited patient contact, referral commissions to clinicians, or infusion arrangements should be made without legal, clinical, professional ethics and Malaysian regulatory review.
