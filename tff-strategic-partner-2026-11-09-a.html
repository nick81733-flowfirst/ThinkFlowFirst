Master Hook Library & Hook Family Taxonomy

Global demand creation & commercialization strategic partner briefing.

Purpose and authoritative source

The existing canonical Master Hook Library is the source of approved, exploratory and locked hook lines. This appendix introduces the logic and is not a replacement for the live hook collection: /hook-family-map.html.

How the library works

A hook family groups reusable motivations rather than generating unrelated one-off slogans. Each creative asset should be tagged by family, exact wording, audience, language, geographic market, claim status, distribution channel and source.

Representative families and examples

Prevention/personal relevance: “This could happen to you. Learn how to prevent it.”
Family navigation: “Before you travel overseas for treatment, Think Flow First.”
Recovery questions: recognition of stalled progress WITHOUT implying a cure.
Curiosity: unanswered questions and evidence-seeking.
Second opinions / next options: informed decisions rather than promises.
Expert voice: clinicians discussing evidence, limitations and uncertainties.
Health optimization: longevity, healthspan, emerging health interests.
Companion therapy: audiences researching stem cells, NAD, PRP, other options.
Community sharing: family members sharing educational material.
Experience and health journey: learn healthy behaviors through experiential programs.

Stroke as first use case

The library should support future independent modules around Parkinson's, Alzheimer's, dementia, cardiac health and metabolic conditions. Reuse creative infrastructure, not disease claims. Disease-specific hooks need scientific and legal review before promotion.

Status and safety

An exploratory statement is not approved advertising. “Seven days is all it takes to turn your health around” is an example of an overly absolute transformation claim needing rewriting. Do not infer viewers' diagnoses or promise treatment results. Require accountable final human signoff.

Why this matters commercially

Winning families can be translated and adapted across source markets with local review, making creative development faster and more measurable. The strategic partner's task is to test actual response quality and ROI, not simply increase the number of hooks.
