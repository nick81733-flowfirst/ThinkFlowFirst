Purpose and decision sequence

We are deliberately setting the earlier ECMP margin discussion aside until we have agreed (1) realistic monthly demand and supply volume, and (2) clear responsibility for each operating function.

The two questions: What volume can Dr. Mah's own clinical and commercial networks reliably deliver, and how confident is he? Which responsibilities will be undertaken by Dr. Mah, his area distributors, FFM/KB, FFG, participating clinics and external providers?

Part 1 — Monthly volume: Dr. Mah's goals and confidence

FFM's objective is a sustainable run rate of at least 100 new customers per month, working on a planning assumption of 20 purchased infusions per customer. Dr. Mah should propose his own achievable targets independently rather than merely adopt FFM's goal.

The 2,000 is a monthly unit-sales planning target at steady state, not a guarantee of treatment administrations in the same calendar month. Package sizes, upgrades and usage timing may differ.

Part 2 — Responsibility matrix

No values or compensation rates are assigned to any responsibility. We first confirm the accountable organization, operational executor, scope, and any legal or practical dependencies. “Shared” must eventually be broken down into a named lead and named support party.

Distinction to preserve: Area distributors (ADs) develop the clinical network, particularly in outlying cities beyond KL. Agents and resellers belong to Dr. Mah's separate commercial network.

Part 3 — What we agree before any compensation negotiation

After the reviews, Dr. Mah and KB should reconcile areas of agreement and disagreements into one operational owner for each function, record service-level expectations, and determine what work must be outsourced or licensed.

  1. Record Dr. Mah's independently achievable Month 3 / 6 / 12 volume targets, confidence and supporting evidence.
  2. Distinguish his network's acquired customers from FFM-generated demand directed to participating clinics.
  3. Confirm clinical-network workflow: Dr. Mah recruits ADs and supports AD-led clinic recruitment outside KL, with direct-clinic exceptions defined.
  4. Confirm separate agent and reseller roles and the systems required to track both.
  5. Assign operational responsibility for importing, regulatory authorization, inventory, logistics, clinic payments, customer attribution and network continuity.
  6. Only then discuss division of work, actual costs and the corresponding ECMP/FFM compensation.
