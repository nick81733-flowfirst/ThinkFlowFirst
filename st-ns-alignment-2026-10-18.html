Dr. Mah's separate clinic distribution model

USD60 to Mah → USD80 to area distributor → USD95 to partner clinic. Introductory patient rate RM4,200 for 5 = RM840 per infusion; standard RM6,000 for 5 = RM1,200 per infusion. If clinic pays 30% agents and USD95 product at RM4.20/USD, remainder before clinic costs = RM189/infusion introductory, RM441 standard. If incremental clinic costs are RM80–120, introductory residual = RM69–109; these costs and who bears commissions require verification. An area distributor may use different acquisition methods.
