Digital Marketing Operating Model, CRM & Analytics

Global demand creation & commercialization strategic partner briefing.

Operating architecture

Central FFG/TFF team owns approved positioning, canonical hook inventory, core analytics, CRM governance and measurement definitions. Local teams adapt languages, channels, influencers, legal rules, licensed provider relationships and follow-up.

Minimum campaign taxonomy

Country, source-market, audience, disease-education category or longevity category, hook ID, family, demand entry door, channel, creator, creative version, CTA, referring partner, campaign budget, start/end, compliance approval and sales attribution.

Lead states

Anonymous exposure → engaged → opted in → meaningful conversation → qualified interest → clinician discussion requested → consultation attended → approved service purchase or future updates. Protect clinical data and separate patient, payer and caregiver roles.

KPIs

Reach, cost per engaged response, cost per meaningful conversation, cost per consented qualified interest (CPQL), consult attendance, customer acquisition cost (CAC), contribution per customer, payback, repeat use and value by destination corridor. Show time delay and unconverted leads.

Human and automated roles

Approved structured content and opt-in acknowledgement may be automated. Clinical triage, medical advice, eligibility and any treatment decision remain with competent professionals. For Khmer or other lower-resource languages, compare menus and human handoff against AI accuracy.

Reporting cadence

Weekly creative and response QA. Monthly country/channel economics and funnel leakage. Quarterly source-market prioritization, regulatory feasibility and provider network readiness. Track claims incidents, privacy incidents and opt-out requests.
