Slide 2 · Where AFW Really Started

A simple founding purpose

AFW began with a simple vision shared by its two founders:

Help people gain better health through wellness.

The original objective was not to build a large corporate platform. It was to create an environment where people could improve their health, recover better, live more healthily and experience a more integrated approach to wellness.

That founding purpose remains unchanged today. What has changed is the opportunity to deliver it at a much greater scale.

Slide 3 · The First Expression of the Vision

That vision became AFW Sanctuary

AFW Sanctuary was developed in Cambodia as a destination for wellness, recovery and healthier living.

Wellness & RecoveryResidential programs designed to help people improve their health and wellbeing.
Property & LifestyleLifestyle real estate surrounding the Sanctuary for people attracted to the environment and wellness philosophy.

Together, these formed the original AFW Sanctuary ecosystem.

Slide 4 · The Original Scale Model

AFW was never intended to depend only on building properties ourselves

Once the Sanctuary model was proven, the ambition was to take the wellness concept into other markets. AFW did not assume every future destination had to be built and owned from the ground up.

Owned FlagshipsAFW-developed destinations where strategically and economically appropriate.
Converted Third-Party PropertiesExisting hotels, resorts or suitable properties adapted into AFW wellness destinations.
PartnershipsWorking with property owners and operators rather than funding every destination ourselves.
Lifestyle Real EstateDeveloping residential and lifestyle opportunities around selected flagship destinations.

Even the original model therefore contained the beginnings of an asset-light expansion strategy.

Slide 5 · Then the Opportunity Expanded

Exclusive access to a new leading-edge therapeutic technology platform created another scaling path

AFW subsequently secured access to an emerging therapeutic technology platform with the potential to complement its wellness philosophy.

Importantly, this access provides AFW with the opportunity to commercialize the platform across markets beyond the Sanctuary itself, subject to local regulatory and commercial arrangements.

  • partner clinics
  • wellness centers
  • dedicated outlets
  • healthcare and referral partners
  • country partners
  • other suitable delivery channels
For the first time, AFW's growth no longer required a wellness destination in every market.
