THINK FLOW FIRST · APPENDIX F · VERSION 1.0 · 9 NOVEMBER 2026

Global Demand Discovery, Prelaunch & Medical Tourism

Global demand creation & commercialization strategic partner briefing.

Worldwide, not regional

Think Flow First is designed for global demand discovery. Southeast Asia is an early operational base, while high-interest international healthcare corridors can be researched concurrently or next: Mexico as a possible US-facing access hub; Türkiye across European, Middle Eastern and neighboring flows; UAE/Gulf as premium healthcare and longevity markets; Europe and East Asia as potential source and local-access regions.

Three market classifications

SOURCE MARKETS generate consumer demand.
DESTINATION MARKETS host appropriately licensed providers serving eligible visitors.
LOCAL COMMERCIALIZATION MARKETS eventually have lawful domestic access.
These roles can overlap, but should never be confused in dashboards or contracts.

How prelaunch demand development works

Begin lawful local-language education even before domestic availability, creating opt-in educational audiences and tracking what questions matter. Measure country, language, topic, source, future-notification demand and willingness to travel. Simultaneously prepare evidence, regulatory and partner due diligence. Avoid implying future access is approved.

Immediate versus future pathways

Path A: where both source- and destination-market rules allow it, an interested person may independently explore a verified overseas provider. Possible destination hypotheses include Indonesia, Malaysia, Cambodia, Mexico, Türkiye and UAE; none should be advertised as an approved Advanced Flow destination without evidence of exact product and provider authorization.
Path B: consented educational and future-availability notifications for eventual domestic markets.
Path C: use accumulated aggregated intelligence to decide where FFG should invest in providers, approvals and partnerships.

Illustrative source-destination corridors

US / Canada → Mexico (investigate US advertising and Mexican provider legality).
Europe / Middle East / Central Asia → Türkiye (assess suitability and practical specialties).
Gulf / international residents → UAE (separate longevity and disease-related interests).
Singapore and ASEAN → Malaysia / Indonesia / Cambodia (verify exact offering and referrals).
Hong Kong, Taiwan, Japan, Korea → possible authorized hubs (feasibility first).

Different levels of demand

Reach ≠ engagement ≠ opt-in ≠ qualified interest ≠ patient booking ≠ paid customer. A country with a large list of curious followers may have weak acquisition economics. Define each metric and measure over time.

Clinical and regulatory safeguards

Cross-border travel never removes home-market promotion rules. Licensed clinicians assess medical fitness to travel; evidence uncertainties, continuity of care, actual provider authorization, informed consent and post-treatment arrangements must be transparent.

Decision output

Market scorecard should rank qualified interest cost, provider readiness, legal feasibility, travel friction, contribution economics and evidence requirements, not just population or medical tourism volume.
