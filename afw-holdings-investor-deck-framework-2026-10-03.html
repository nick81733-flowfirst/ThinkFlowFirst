Slide 22 · Balance Sheet Strategy

Resolve the Past Without Starving the Future

AFW currently has approximately USD 550,000 of borrowings that management would like to restructure, refinance or repay so the business can focus on growth.

The objective is not to automatically use scarce equity capital to retire every dollar of debt.

Option 1 — Restructure / RefinanceExtend tenure, reduce immediate pressure and preserve equity capital for growth.
Option 2 — Strategic CapitalUse part of the raise to strengthen the balance sheet where it creates the greatest operating benefit.
Key principle: If strategic investors can help solve debt through refinancing, banking relationships or structured capital, the amount of new equity required may be lower.

Slide 26 · Contribution Discipline

Strategic Value Must Be Measurable

AFW welcomes strategic contribution, but cash, services, assets and executive responsibility should be valued separately.

  • Cash invested — priced as investment capital.
  • Services contributed — defined scope, deliverables and commercial value.
  • Assets or infrastructure contributed — independently valued where appropriate.
  • Executive responsibility — tied to role, accountability and performance.
Strategic value can influence allocation — but equity should not be exchanged for vague promises.
