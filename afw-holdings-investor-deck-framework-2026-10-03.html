11 · Replication Logic

1,000 Customers a Month — Built 10 at a Time

Across 100 productive outlets, each outlet needs only 10 new customers per month — roughly one new customer every three days.

Scale comes from replication, not extraordinary performance from individual outlets.

13 · Strategic Upside 1

Indonesia Tourism — Capital-Light Regional Upside

Indonesia may become an early-access hub for regional customers seeking advanced wellness experiences. AFW can participate through customer acquisition, packaged journeys, referrals and partner economics without building a large new owned asset base.

Positioning: Indonesia Tourism is strategic upside. It is not required for the base investment economics.
14 · Strategic Upside 2

Biosciences — Long-Term Partnerable Asset

Over time, structured evidence, regulatory readiness, data and intellectual-property development may create a biotech asset capable of licensing, co-development or strategic partnership.

Positioning: Biosciences is longer-term asymmetric upside. No value from it is required to justify the current base-case operating thesis.

17 · The Resource Gap

The Opportunity Has Outgrown Our Current Resources

AFW deliberately operated lean to prove the business on the ground. The organization required to prove an opportunity is not the same organization required to scale it.

TodayFounder-led execution, lean team, existing Sanctuary, early commercial traction, technology access and emerging regional relationships.
Scale RequiresStronger management, growth capital, balance-sheet restructuring, demand capability, distribution expertise, real-estate commercialization and stronger operating systems.
18 · Management

Strengthen the Team Around the Founders

AFW can operate the Sanctuary. The broader opportunity requires additional leadership around Cambodia commercialization, regional distribution, digital demand generation, finance, property development and group execution.

The objective is not to replace founder capability. It is to build the management depth required by a much larger opportunity.
19 · Balance Sheet

Resolve the Past Without Starving the Future

AFW has approximately USD 550,000 of borrowings that management wants to restructure, refinance or repay so attention can remain on the opportunity ahead.

The strategy is not to assume every dollar must be repaid from equity capital.

Refinance / RestructureExtend tenure, reduce near-term pressure and preserve growth capital.
Selective RepaymentUse strategic capital where repayment creates meaningful operating benefit.
