1 · Founding Purpose

The mission did not change. The opportunity became much bigger.

AFW began with a simple vision shared by its two founders: help people gain better health through wellness. That vision became AFW Sanctuary in Cambodia, combining wellness and recovery with a lifestyle-property ecosystem.

The original scale model was already flexible: AFW could own selected flagships, convert third-party hotels or resorts into AFW wellness destinations, and work with property owners and operators rather than build every location itself.

Founding purpose → Sanctuary + Property → Asset-light destination expansion → Therapeutic technology access → Cambodia Commercial + Global Platform.
2 · Structural Transformation

Health, wellness and longevity are becoming major long-term investment themes

McKinsey’s Future of Wellness work estimates the global consumer wellness market at about US$2 trillion and identifies healthy aging and in-person wellness services among categories attracting growing investor interest, M&A activity and innovation.

McKinsey Health Institute also frames improving health as a major economic opportunity. Its 2026 work estimates that scaling proven health interventions could create roughly US$12.5 trillion in annual global economic value by 2050, with prevention accounting for much of the opportunity.

Consumer shiftConsumers are taking greater control of health and increasingly expect effective, science-backed, personalized solutions.
Healthy agingLonger lives are increasing demand for healthspan, function, prevention and longevity services.
From reactive to proactiveHealth systems and consumers are moving beyond disease treatment toward prevention and ongoing wellness management.
Investment relevanceHealthy aging and in-person wellness services are areas of growing investor interest and deal activity.
Important: McKinsey’s economy-wide return figures are not AFW investor returns. They support the structural sector thesis only.

Sources: McKinsey & Company, Future of Wellness 2025; McKinsey Health Institute, The Health of Nations 2026; Healthy Longevity research.

25 · Investor Returns

Two potential sources of investor return

1 · Cash distributionsAs operating businesses mature and generate free cash flow, AFW may distribute part of available cash to shareholders, subject to profitability, debt service, working capital, reinvestment needs, board approval and applicable law.
2 · Equity value appreciationIf AFW successfully scales its operating, platform and property engines, the value of the investor’s ownership stake may increase over time. Realization would require a secondary sale, strategic acquisition, company buyback or other liquidity event.
The investment thesis is not only “sell later.” It can combine current cash yield with longer-term ownership appreciation.

28 · Combined Return Logic

Cash yield + ownership appreciation

The total investor outcome over time can be thought of as:

Total value received = cumulative cash distributions + value realized from the investor’s equity stake.

An investor could therefore receive periodic cash distributions while continuing to own the same shares, subject to dilution from future capital raises. Equity value is only realized when liquidity occurs.

For formal investor materials, return examples should always be labeled illustrative and should not imply guaranteed dividends, valuation, timing or exit.

34 · Regulatory & Claims Discipline

Scale requires discipline as much as ambition

Lead with appropriate wellness, recovery, prevention, optimization and longevity positioning; use qualified professionals and compliant facilities; treat each market independently; and separate disease-treatment claims from general wellness pathways.

35 · Commercial Rights & Governance

The platform must be contractually bankable

Due diligence should cover territory, exclusivity conditions, excluded markets, supply continuity, transfer pricing, brand/training/quality rights, technology improvements, data rights, partner performance and termination mechanisms. Before using the word “exclusive” in investor materials, confirm that the underlying agreements support the exact territorial and channel scope claimed.
