1 · The Investment Story

AFW Has Reached an Inflection Point

AFW started by building and operating a destination wellness business. That process created land, facilities, staff capability, operating experience, customer learning and a practical understanding of what people will pay for.

At the same time, access to Advanced Flow technology, the development of FlowFirst Cambodia, regional partner interest, the demand-generation strategy and the property opportunity have expanded AFW far beyond a single Sanctuary business.

We are no longer trying to prove whether there is an opportunity. We are trying to assemble the resources required to capture it.

12 · AFW Sanctuary Strategic Positioning

Recovery · Prevention · Longevity

AFW Sanctuary should not be defined by its initial program menu. The broader strategic role is to become a residential Recovery, Prevention and Longevity hub for Cambodia and the wider ASEAN market.

RecoverySupport people rebuilding function after illness, hospitalization, treatment, neurological events or periods of severe deconditioning.
PreventionHelp higher-risk customers improve vascular, metabolic, movement, sleep, nutrition and lifestyle factors before major decline occurs.
LongevityServe customers focused on healthy aging, function, resilience, performance, healthspan and long-term optimization.
Recovery brings urgency. Prevention broadens the market. Longevity extends the relationship.

15 · Specialist Stroke Recovery Opportunity

A Premium Residential Stroke-Recovery Center

Stroke remains one of the strongest specialist opportunities for AFW because the Sanctuary already has the residential environment, privacy, nursing support, food, recovery setting and potential vascular-first differentiation.

The goal is not to position AFW as another outpatient rehabilitation clinic. The opportunity is a premium residential stroke-recovery campus where rehabilitation, living environment, nutrition, caregiver support and complementary vascular-health services are integrated.

RehabilitationPhysiotherapy, occupational therapy, speech/swallow support, mobility and activities-of-daily-living training.
Residential SupportAccommodation, nursing coordination, structured routines, food, movement and caregiver support.
MeasurementBaseline assessment, goals, weekly progress tracking and outcome reporting.
16 · Stroke-Recovery Partner Model

Build the Specialist Capability Through a Rehabilitation Partner

AFW should not try to build the entire rehabilitation organization alone. The preferred model is to secure a strong rehabilitation partner that can own the specialist clinical and therapy layer.

AFW ProvidesAccommodation, food, nursing coordination, hospitality, customer acquisition, caregiver experience, FlowFirst/Advanced Flow services where appropriate, and the Sanctuary environment.
Rehabilitation Partner ProvidesTherapists, rehabilitation protocols, clinical leadership, equipment, therapy scheduling, outcome measures and professional standards.

This allows AFW to create a specialist residential product without carrying the full staffing, equipment and clinical-complexity burden from day one.

Illustrative partner economics previously explored: around US$500/day average patient revenue, with roughly 20% / US$100 per occupied patient-day allocated to the rehabilitation partner. At three occupied patients, that would equal around US$9,000/month to the partner. Final economics should be negotiated from the actual service scope.
17 · Why Stroke Can Be the First Specialist Wedge

A Focused Door Into a Much Larger Recovery Market

Stroke has several characteristics that make it a practical first specialist wedge: visible unmet need, long recovery timelines, family involvement, willingness to travel for better options, and the possibility of measuring functional progress.

Urgent DemandFamilies actively search for additional options when recovery plateaus.
Residential FitMany patients benefit from structured daily therapy, nursing support and caregiver education.
Expandable ModelThe same residential operating platform can later support Parkinson’s, frailty, post-hospital recovery and other neurological or functional pathways.
Stroke can be the specialist entry point. The Sanctuary remains a broader Recovery · Prevention · Longevity platform.
