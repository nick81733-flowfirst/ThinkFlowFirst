1 · The Investment Story

AFW Has Reached an Inflection Point

AFW started by building and operating a destination wellness business. That process created land, facilities, staff capability, operating experience, customer learning and a practical understanding of what people will pay for.

At the same time, access to Advanced Flow technology, the development of FlowFirst Cambodia, regional partner interest, the demand-generation strategy and the property opportunity have expanded AFW far beyond a single Sanctuary business.

We are no longer trying to prove whether there is an opportunity. We are trying to assemble the resources required to capture it.

6 · Why the Opportunity Is Larger Than the Current Organisation

The Resource Gap

AFW has deliberately been operating lean. That was appropriate while proving the model. It is no longer sufficient for the scale of the opportunity now visible.

What We Have TodayFounder-led execution, lean operating team, functioning Sanctuary, property base, early commercial traction, technology access, emerging demand platform and partner relationships.
What Scale RequiresStronger management, growth capital, debt restructuring, digital demand capability, distribution expertise, real-estate commercialization, partner systems, data, governance and operating discipline.
The organisation that proves an opportunity is not necessarily the organisation that can exploit it at scale.
7 · Management

AFW can run the Sanctuary. The broader platform requires additional senior capability around Cambodia commercialization, regional distribution, digital demand, finance, property development, governance and group execution.

The goal is not to replace the founders. It is to surround them with the operating depth required by a much larger business.

10 · Contribution Discipline

Strategic Value Must Be Measurable

  • Cash invested — priced as investment capital.
  • Services contributed — defined scope, deliverables and commercial value.
  • Assets or infrastructure contributed — separately valued where appropriate.
  • Executive responsibility — tied to role, accountability and performance.
Strategic value can influence allocation, but equity should not be exchanged for vague promises.
11 · Balance Sheet

Resolve the Past Without Starving the Future

AFW currently has approximately US$550K of borrowings that management wants to restructure, refinance or repay so attention can remain on growth.

This does not mean US$550K of equity capital must automatically be used for debt repayment.

Restructure / RefinanceExtend tenure, reduce near-term pressure and preserve growth capital.
Selective RepaymentUse equity only where repayment materially improves focus or resilience.
Strategic opportunity: an investor with banking relationships or structured-finance capability can potentially reduce the amount of equity AFW needs to raise.

32 · Regulatory and Claims Discipline

Scale Requires Claims Discipline as Much as Ambition

  • Lead with health-optimization positioning where appropriate.
  • Use qualified local professionals and compliant facilities.
  • Treat each country independently; do not assume one market’s classification applies elsewhere.
  • Separate disease-treatment pathways from general wellness/optimization pathways.
  • Clinical claims require appropriate evidence and authorization.
