03 · Commission structure & simple tracker

Any agent can build a team; each sale pays two levels

Patricia requested 15% for the customer’s introducing agent and 5% for their immediate supervising agent. An agent may become a supervisor by introducing other agents. The network can have many generations, while each sale pays only two levels.

PackageIntroducing agent: 15%Immediate supervisor: 5%FlowFirst before costs
S$1,000S$150S$50S$800
S$2,300S$345S$115S$1,840
S$4,200S$630S$210S$3,360

Example: Patricia introduces A; A introduces B. When B introduces the customer, B earns 15%, A earns 5%, and Patricia receives no payment on that sale. When A introduces a customer directly, A earns 15% and Patricia earns 5%.

A three-level alternative of 15% + 3% + 2% was explored but not adopted. Total commission remains 20% in the working launch model. This Batam-specific proposal does not amend AFW Sanctuary’s separate commission policy.

Keep customer entry to four fields

Name · date of birth · mobile phone · email. The system also records the introducing agent, immediate supervisor, package, payment, delivered sessions and next follow-up. Agent records need name, contact, agent code and immediate supervisor. Medical records remain with the clinical provider.

Central records allow follow-up if an agent becomes inactive. Proposed access: agents see their own customers; supervisors see relevant team records; the central team maintains continuity. Limit access appropriately and record contact permission.

Rules still to agree: payout timing, refunds, absent supervisor, duplicate leads, attribution on upgrades and reassignment. Proposed defaults were commission on collected therapy payments, adjustments for refunds, and upgrade commission only on additional money. Two-level mechanics are not legal clearance: the actual Singapore agent arrangement needs review against applicable rules.

08 · Singapore clinician-led pathway

Special access is an individual clinical decision

The discussed pathway assumes HSA classifies IGDS as an unregistered therapeutic product. Confirm classification first. A sponsoring clinician reviews the evidence and patient, documents why registered alternatives are insufficient or why the proposed product offers a substantive clinical advantage, obtains consent and signs the Named-Patient request. Investigational products may require documented local peer consensus for the specific situation.

An eligible institution, retail pharmacy or licensed importer submits through Corppass. Each consignment needs prior HSA approval. HSA publishes a 14-working-day target excluding clarification pauses, no application fee, six-month named-patient consignment approval validity, and normally a maximum three-month supply per patient. These are import-access provisions, not HSA endorsement of product effectiveness.

FlowFirst/RAHO should assemble composition, labels, manufacturing/quality, sterility, storage, clinical and safety information for the reviewers. Existing infusion services solve delivery capacity only after lawful access and clinical acceptance are established.

For stroke, prioritize neurologists, rehabilitation physicians, geriatricians and relevant family/homecare medical directors. A proposed first outreach list is 20–30 selected clinicians. Invite independent review, including objections and missing evidence; no stroke outcome or prescribing commitment should be assumed.

Batam participants can be offered a private referral for Singapore assessment where relevant. Prior Batam treatment does not automatically qualify them for special access. Unregistered special-access products cannot be advertised to the public; prescribing clinicians may discuss individual options with patients. Ordinary word of mouth does not make organized promotion exempt.

Demand for Singaporeans receiving IV drips in JB was not quantified. Existing providers and wider medical tourism support investigation, but do not justify a forecast of substantial Advanced Flow demand. Ask partners for aggregate customer volumes, repeat visits and package uptake.
