Singapore Internationalisation Funding — Internal Appendix

Internal research appendix — exclude from normal external sharing.

Purpose

This appendix is for FFG internal financial planning and potential Enterprise Singapore consultation. The external main briefing should make only a brief mention of possible Singapore support. Grant or tax incentives do not authorize prohibited medical product promotion.

MRA versus EDGE: material distinction

Former MRA support used a S$100,000 per company per new-market maximum, subject to activity caps and approval. The current EDGE framework uses S$100,000 per company annually across eligible EDGE activities, not per overseas country. The grant year resets on 1 April. Several countries may be applied for, but their EDGE support shares that annual company-wide ceiling. Confirm rates and transitional provisions against current official FAQs.

EDGE — overseas marketing / PR

Most relevant for eligible locally adapted overseas campaigns and professional services. Not every ad budget, influencer expense or central platform cost automatically qualifies. Applicant ownership, Singapore business operation, qualifying overseas sales objective, pre-application timing and vendor documentation matter.

EDGE — market entry setup

Potentially relevant to overseas establishment, agreements, licensing and company setup, but uses the same company-wide EDGE annual cap.

Double Tax Deduction for Internationalisation (DTDi)

Tax incentive rather than cash reimbursement, potentially relevant for qualifying overseas promotion, market surveys and other expenses. IRAS rules apply, including calculations net of funded expenditure. Actual value depends on taxable profits.

Global Innovation Alliance (GIA+)

Possible only for qualifying Singapore-based tech startups and approved market access programs; not a general advertising grant. Evaluate if the TFF technology platform becomes an eligible standalone venture.

Other schemes

BizAdapt appears conditional on specified tariff-related impacts and should not be assumed relevant. Further suitable innovation/productivity schemes should be evaluated only after FFG's exact Singapore entity, ownership, product rights and project scope are documented.

Proposed costing buckets

Overseas country-specific market research; overseas marketing and PR vendors; clinical partner identification; legal/regulatory and entry setup; shared CRM/software; taxable overhead and qualifying DTDi items. Separate invoices and avoid double funding.
